Hi — thanks for helping test Snapdrop.

This build has a feedback button: a small blue bubble in the corner. If anything
looks wrong, tap it, tap the part of the screen you mean, and type what you saw.
That is the whole thing. You do not need to describe your phone, your Android
version, or which screen you were on — it sends all of that with your comment.

**One thing to know before you use it.** When you tap send, it uploads a picture
of the screen you are on. If your file list is showing, that picture includes
your file names and thumbnails. Your actual files are never uploaded — they still
go straight between your devices. Nothing is sent unless you tap send, and you
can ask me to delete any of your feedback, or all of it, at any time.

If something is broken enough that you cannot reach the button, message me
normally.

The version on the Play Store does not have any of this in it.
