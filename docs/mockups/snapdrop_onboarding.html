Snapdrop

Send photos straight into Figma

Snapdrop pushes images from your phone into your Figma canvas — through the desktop plugin.

1

Open Snapdrop in Figma on your computer

The desktop plugin shows a QR code on your screen.

2

Pick your photos, then scan that QR

Point your phone at the code to connect.

3

Your photos drop into Figma

They appear in your canvas, ready to use.

The QR code comes from the desktop Figma plugin — not this app, and not Figma mobile.
