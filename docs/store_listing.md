## en-US

### Title
Snapdrop: Phone to Figma

### Short description
Send photos from your phone straight into Figma. Scan a QR. No cables, no login.

### Full description
Snapdrop sends photos from your phone straight into your Figma file. No cables, no cloud drive, no emailing yourself screenshots.

You need the free Snapdrop plugin open in Figma on your computer. It shows the QR code you scan.

HOW IT WORKS
1. On your computer, open Figma and run the Snapdrop plugin. It shows a QR code.
2. On your phone, open Snapdrop and choose up to 10 photos.
3. Tap Connect and scan the QR code. The photos land on your canvas in seconds.

Stay connected and keep sending. Scan once, then send more photos without scanning again.

BUILT FOR DESIGNERS
• Reference photos and moodboards
• Product shots and textures for mockups
• Hand-drawn sketches and whiteboard photos
• Phone screenshots for UI reviews and bug reports

WHY SNAPDROP
• Fast: pick, scan, done
• No account, no sign-up, no login
• Private: photos go straight to your Figma session and are not stored on our servers
• No gallery permission: you choose photos with Android's own photo picker
• Share to Snapdrop from your gallery or any app
• In 7 languages: English, Português, Español, Français, हिन्दी, 中文, العربية

CAN'T FIND THE QR CODE?
The QR code comes from the Snapdrop plugin in Figma on a desktop or laptop, not from Figma's mobile app. Tap "Where's the QR code?" in the app for a step-by-step guide.

Works with Figma in the desktop app or the browser on Mac, Windows, Linux and ChromeOS.

More at getsnapdrop.in

Snapdrop is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Figma.
